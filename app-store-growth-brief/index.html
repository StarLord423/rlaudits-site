Quick answer

What is an App Store Growth Brief?

An App Store Growth Brief is a public-evidence review of one app listing that turns store-page copy, review themes, competitor contrast, and release-note signals into prioritized listing and trust fixes.

The deliverable

What comes in the brief.

Every brief contains public evidence, source labels, exact recommended fixes, competitor context when inspectable, and a prioritized 30-day sequence.

Listing copy
Exact store-surface fixes
Subtitle, short description, first long-description paragraph, pricing/pro table, support FAQ, or release-note changes.
Review friction
Reply templates
Public review themes become support-safe reply templates and escalation language without reviewer identity guesses.
Competitors
Positioning map
Compare public store positioning against a compact competitor set to amplify stronger claims and remove weak ones.
Cross-store gap
Apple vs Google Play
Where trust signals are stronger, weaker, or inconsistent. If evidence is thin, the report says so.
30-day plan
Action sequence
Listing edits, reply backlog, release-note cleanup, source refresh, and follow-up checks.
Boundaries
Source-confidence labels
No private analytics, App Store Connect, Play Console, paid scraping, or outcome guarantees by default.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask before paying.

Do you need App Store Connect or Play Console access?

No for the initial brief. The first paid report uses public Apple App Store and Google Play evidence when feasible. Private account access is optional later for implementation workflows, not required to order.

What if the app is Apple-only or Android-only?

We still inspect cross-store availability. If the other store has no useful public counterpart, too little evidence, or is intentionally out of scope, the brief states that boundary instead of pretending the evidence is complete.

What is the delivery promise?

RLAudits first reviews whether the public listing has enough evidence for a useful brief. If accepted and paid, standard delivery target is three business days after clean intake and payment.

What is refundable?

Payment is requested only after fit review. If RLAudits cannot produce a useful source-backed brief after accepting payment because public evidence is unavailable or too thin, the order is refunded or converted into a different target before work starts.

Does the brief guarantee better rankings or installs?

No. The brief gives evidence-backed listing, review, positioning, and release-note recommendations. Store rankings, installs, revenue, ratings, and policy outcomes are not guaranteed.
