01 / Executive summary

The app looks useful, but the listing makes buyers work too hard.

Owner-ready summary

Sample Meal Planner has a clear product idea: help busy households plan meals, build grocery lists, and reduce weeknight decision fatigue. The current public listing explains features, but it does not quickly prove the outcome or show the workflow in the first two screenshots.

The strongest near-term move is to reposition the listing around the user job: plan the week, auto-build the grocery list, and adjust when plans change. That message should show up in the title/subtitle, first screenshot, first paragraph, and review-response themes.

This brief recommends a practical refresh: tighten metadata, reorder screenshots by decision value, clarify family-sharing and subscription language, mine review themes for trust fixes, and compare against stronger competitor listing patterns without copying their brand.

05 / Title, subtitle, and description rewrite suggestions

Rewrite around the job, not the feature pile.

Title / subtitle

Current sample pattern

Meal Planner App — Recipes, Lists & Healthy Food

Recommended direction

Weekly Meal Planner — Dinner Plans & Grocery Lists

First paragraph

Current sample pattern

Organize recipes, create meals, and manage groceries with an easy planner for busy people.

Recommended direction

Plan a week of dinners, turn the plan into a grocery list, and adjust when family schedules change. Sample Meal Planner helps busy households decide what to cook before the 5 p.m. scramble starts.

Listing elementRewrite ruleWhy it matters
TitleKeep category and primary job visible.Users and store searchers need to understand the app before admiring wordplay.
SubtitleUse a second concrete benefit: grocery lists, family planning, or dinner decisions.Subtitle should support the title, not repeat it wearing a hat.
Description introLead with audience, outcome, and workflow.The first paragraph is where vague productivity copy goes to die.
Feature bulletsGroup by buyer need: plan, shop, adjust, share, upgrade.Feature lists should guide decisions, not inventory every button.

07 / Trust and review notes

Trust gaps are usually small, visible, and fixable.

Review themes
Positive theme
Useful

Users like saving time and reducing meal decisions. Move that phrase into screenshots and opening copy.

Concern theme
Sync

If reviews mention sync or sharing issues, release notes and support replies need plain status updates.

Trust theme
Pro

Subscription value must be explained before users hit upgrade friction.

Recommended trust fixes

Next
  • Add one description bullet explaining free vs Pro in plain language.
  • Use release notes to address sync, import, and sharing improvements when they ship.
  • Reply to repeat review concerns with specific, non-defensive support paths.
  • Add a support/contact line near subscription and account language.
  • Avoid implying health, savings, or household outcomes that the app does not directly support.
No-guarantee note: Better trust language may reduce confusion, but this report does not guarantee review changes, rating improvement, rankings, downloads, trials, subscriptions, retention, or revenue.
