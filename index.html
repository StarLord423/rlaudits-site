FAQ

Short answers before you ask.

What is an RL Audits report?

An RL Audits report is a private, evidence-first briefing that turns public signals or sanitized operational data into ranked fixes and a 30-day action plan.

Do you need passwords, analytics access, or private accounts?

No by default. Standard audits use public evidence or sanitized material. If deeper access is ever useful, RLAudits discusses a safer process separately.

Who are these audits for?

They are for small local businesses, small IT teams, MSPs, clinics, agencies, and small app teams that need specific fixes instead of vague consulting advice.

Does RL Audits guarantee rankings, revenue, or compliance outcomes?

No. The work identifies evidence-backed issues and practical next steps. Rankings, revenue, ratings, legal compliance, and security outcomes are not guaranteed.
