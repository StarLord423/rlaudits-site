Quick answer

What is a Revenue Audit?

A Revenue Audit is a private review of the visible sales path: reviews, trust signals, website clarity, contact friction, local competitors, and the next steps most likely to improve buyer confidence.

The deliverable

This is what you receive.

A private, boardroom-grade report built from public reviews, website friction, trust signals, and local competitors. Not a spreadsheet. Not a slide deck. A ranked action plan your team can actually use.

Position snapshot
Where you actually stand
Four pillars scored 0–100 against your local benchmark. Radar chart plus scorecard with trend indicators.
Revenue leak map
Where the funnel breaks
Five-stage path from discovery to call, booking, quote request, or repeat visit. Every leak pinned to a specific ranked fix.
Competitive analysis
How you compare locally
3–5 direct competitors mapped by positioning, service mix, and trust signals. What they do well and what to take.
Ranked fixes
Prioritized by revenue impact
Each fix has a why, a how, and an impact priority. Ordered from highest to lowest value so you know exactly where to start.
30-day action plan
A month, broken into workstreams
Four parallel workstreams mapped across four weeks. Color-coded and sequenced so your team can execute immediately.
45-min debrief call
Walk through it together
We review every finding live, answer your questions, and help you prioritize where to put your energy first.
